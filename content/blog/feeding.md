# Feeding your bees

## Warning

Sugar syrup WILL ferment, and make your bees sick.  Take care with leaving sugar syrup in your hive.

## Notes on Sugar Syrup

* 2:1 sugar syrup
  * 16lbs sugar to 1 gallon water is 2.1 gallons of 2:1
* 1:1 sugar syrup
  * 8lbs sugar to 1 gallon water is 1.6 gallons of 1:1
  * Approximately 1/8 gallon (1 pint) water per pound of sugar

Ratios are approximate, the bees don't mind if the syrup is 2.1:1 or 1.9:1.
1:1 equal parts water and sugar. 2:1 means twice the weight of sugar to water

## Spring Feeding

Feeding 1:1 in the spring promotes comb production in spring.
