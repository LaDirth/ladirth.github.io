# Feeding your bees

## Notes on Sugar Syrup

Following ratios fit in a 6 quart pot

* 2:1 is half a gallon of water to 8lbs of sugar

* 1:1 is half a gallon of water to 4lbs of sugar
  * Ends up with approximately 1.6 gallons of 1:1

Ratios are approximate, the bees don't mine 2.1:1 or 1.9:1 syrup.
1:1 equal parts water and sugar. 2:1 means twice the sugar to water

### Handy Ratios

* 2:1 has a ratio of 2.1
  * 16lbs sugar to 1 gallon water is 2.1 gallons of 2:1
* 1:1 has a ratio of 1.6
  * 8lbs sugar to 1 gallon water is 1.6 gallons of !:1
* Approximately 1/8 gallon (1 pint) water per pound of sugar

## Spring Feeding

Feeding 1:1 in the spring promotes comb production in spring.
